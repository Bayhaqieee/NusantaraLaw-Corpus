# Nusantara Law Corpus

## Overview

The Nusantara Law Corpus is a comprehensive collection of Indonesian legal reference materials and datasets, designed to support legal research, education, and natural language processing applications.

## Purpose

This repository aggregates authoritative Indonesian legal terminology, constitutional documents, and judicial guidelines to provide:

* Standardized legal terminology definitions
* Constitutional reference materials
* Legal precedents and judicial guidelines
* Multilingual legal glossaries

## Contents

### Datasets

| Dataset | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **UUD** | Indonesian Constitution (Undang-Undang Dasar) |
| **LawDictionary** | Comprehensive Indonesian legal dictionary with alphabetical entries (A-Z) |
| **HukumOnline** | Comprehensive Indonesian legal dictionary with alphabetical entries (A-Z) |
| **Glosarium-MA** | Glossary from Constitutional Court (Mahkamah Agung) |
| **KHPTSultra** | Judicial guidelines and court precedents |
| **Adagium** | Legal maxims and Latin legal principles |
| **GBHN** | State Policy Guidelines (Garis-garis Besar Haluan Negara) 1999-2004, outlining national development goals and policies established by the MPR |
| **TAP MPR** | Decrees of the People's Consultative Assembly (Ketetapan MPR), including resolutions on state policy and constitutional matters |

## Data Format

Each dataset contains:

* Individual JSON files organized alphabetically (where applicable)
* `all.json` - Complete compiled dataset
* `README.md` - Dataset-specific documentation

## Use Cases

* Legal research and terminology lookup
* Training datasets for NLP and legal AI models
* Educational resources for law students
* Standardization of legal terminology
